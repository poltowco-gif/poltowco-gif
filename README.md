## 👨‍🔬 About Me

I'm a **PhD researcher and engineer** working at the intersection of **wireless energy transfer, power electronics, RF/electromagnetics, and embedded systems**, with a background in **robotics and computer vision**. Before moving into research, I worked as a **3GPP PS Core engineer** in telecom.

My goal is to help build **clean mobility** through efficient wireless charging and **intelligent perception** for autonomous systems.

## 🔭 Focus Areas

| | Area | What I work on |
|---|------|----------------|
| ⚡ | **EV Wireless Power Transfer** | Coil design, compensation topologies, power conversion, efficiency optimization |
| 📡 | **RF & Electromagnetics** | Field simulation, coupled structures, antenna and shielding analysis |
| 🔧 | **Embedded & FPGA** | Real-time control, digital design, hardware acceleration |
| 🤖 | **Robotics + Computer Vision** | Perception pipelines for intelligent, autonomous systems |
| 📶 | **Telecom Core** | Ex-3GPP PS Core engineering experience |
